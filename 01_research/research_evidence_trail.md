# Integrative Health Intelligence — Research Evidence Trail

## What was already established
The project began with the problem space of recurring/confusing health concerns, fragmented information and conflicting explanations. Research was used to move away from a generic symptom checker toward a health-reasoning / sense-making product.

## Key product insight
The opportunity is not another symptom checker, chatbot or integrative consultation marketplace. The differentiated layer is transparent reasoning: structure the user story, show possible contributors without diagnosing, expose evidence provenance and uncertainty, compare explanatory frameworks without false equivalence, identify missing information, route safety issues, and produce a clinician-facing decision brief.

## Research → product decisions
- **Need:** users encounter fragmented/conflicting explanations → build a structured Health Story and Evidence Lens.
- **Trust problem:** users need to know what can actually be trusted → show evidence tier, source and limitations.
- **Causality risk:** temporal association can be mistaken for cause → Contributor Map uses cautious language and preserves uncertainty.
- **Framework conflict:** biomedical, Ayurveda and Homeopathy can be presented together online → separate frameworks and label their evidence basis rather than implying equivalence.
- **Safety:** routine exploration should not continue through urgent patterns → Safety Router interrupts exploration.
- **Decision gap:** information alone does not answer “what do I do next?” → Decision Brief converts the reasoning into questions/actions to discuss with a clinician.

## Evidence discipline
This portfolio distinguishes source-backed research from product hypotheses. No claim in this case study should be read as clinical validation.
