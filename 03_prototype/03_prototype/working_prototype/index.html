FRAMEWORK COMPARISON

Why can different health systems describe the same problem differently?

We keep the perspectives separate so you can see what each framework actually supports.

Biomedical

Evidence-led clinical view

Looks for mechanisms, established risk factors, differential explanations and evidence-supported evaluation.

Evidence basis: Guidelines / reviews / clinical studies
Ayurveda

Traditional framework

May describe symptoms through concepts such as dosha balance and individual constitution. These concepts are presented as a traditional framework, not as biomedical fact.

Evidence basis: Traditional texts + varying research evidence
Homeopathy

Distinct traditional system

Uses its own conceptual model. Claims are not treated as equivalent to established biomedical evidence.

Evidence basis: Limited / insufficient evidence for many clinical claims
