## Source cards

### R01 — WHO India: Diabetes

Verified: 26 Sep 2026
Use: India diabetes population context only.
Evidence note: WHO India states an estimate of 77 million adults with type 2 diabetes and nearly 25 million people with prediabetes, with more than 50% unaware of their status. This is population-level context, not individual diagnosis or risk prediction.

## Retrieval discipline

1. Evidence-required cases must expose a source when making a substantive sourced claim.
2. A source card does not prove the user's individual claim; population context and individual inference remain separate.
3. If a source does not support a requested claim, the model must say so rather than stretch the source.
4. Framework sources establish evidence-policy context, not efficacy of a specific traditional treatment.
