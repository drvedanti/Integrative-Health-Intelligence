# Product Artifact Index

## Core product
**Integrative Health Intelligence — Health Concern Engine**

### Journey
Talk → Structure → Explore → Evidence → Compare → Check uncertainty → Safety → Decide → Follow up

### Core screens / capabilities
1. Welcome + language + consent
2. Tell your story
3. Your Health Story
4. Contributor Map
5. Evidence Lens
6. Missing Information
7. Safety Router
8. Framework Comparison
9. Decision Brief
10. Follow-up

### Product decisions worth inspecting
- Contributor Map instead of diagnosis/root-cause claims.
- Evidence Lens with provenance and evidence tiers.
- Explicit uncertainty and missing-information states.
- Framework comparison that separates biomedical evidence from traditional frameworks.
- Safety-first interruption.
- Story correction / preservation of timing and negation.
- Decision Brief designed around the next clinician conversation.

## Existing build evidence
The evidence package includes the previously built working HTML prototype and public website. These are packaged copies of the existing artifacts, not a reconstruction.

## Scope boundary
The MVP is one complete end-to-end reasoning journey with reusable interaction patterns. It is a portfolio/product prototype, not a clinical decision-support system.
