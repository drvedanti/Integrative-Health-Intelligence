About IHI

Built around a simple question.

What if understanding your health started with understanding the question you are actually trying to ask?

Why IHI exists

Health information can answer “What is it?” without always helping people understand “Why might this be happening?”

IHI was created to make that exploration more understandable: start with a person's own experience, ask better questions, choose a framework, and build an explanation step by step.

Meet the creator

VS

Dr. Vedanti Shah

Dentist · Healthcare × Product × AI

IHI grew from my interest in what happens when clinical understanding, different health frameworks, product thinking and AI are brought together around a very human need: wanting to understand what is happening to you.

I built IHI as an exploration of how health information could become more conversational, contextual and easier to understand.
