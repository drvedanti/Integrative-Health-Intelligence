INTEGRATIVE HEALTH INTELLIGENCE

Make sense of what you're feeling.

Health information can give you answers without helping you understand the story behind them. IHI helps you explore that story through different health frameworks — in plain language.

What is IHI?

One health experience. Different ways of understanding it. IHI helps you explore a health concern through Modern Medicine, Ayurveda or Homeopathy — one approach at a time.

Why does IHI exist?

Because “What is it?” isn't always the question people are really asking. Sometimes the real question is: “Why is this happening to me?”

How does IHI work?

01 · Your experience

Start with what you are noticing, in your own words.

02 · Questions

IHI asks questions relevant to what you told it.

03 · A framework

Choose how you want to explore the experience.

04 · Explanation

Understand what may be happening and why.

05 · Ask deeper

Keep asking questions without losing your context.

06 · What next

Explore practical steps, what to avoid and safety information.

ABOUT IHI

Why IHI?

IHI began with an observation from working with patients: people often want more than an answer. They want to understand what is happening and why.
