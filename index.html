Three perspectives.

These frameworks ask different questions. They are shown separately rather than treated as having the same evidence base.

CLINICAL PERSPECTIVE

Modern Medicine

Looks at symptoms, history, examination and tests where appropriate. The focus is on explanations supported by clinical evidence and on ruling out important causes.

TRADITIONAL FRAMEWORK

Ayurveda

Uses its own traditional concepts for understanding health and imbalance. These concepts should be understood as a traditional framework, not automatically as clinical evidence.

TRADITIONAL FRAMEWORK

Homeopathy

Uses a distinct traditional framework for interpreting symptoms. Its explanations should not be presented as equivalent to evidence-based clinical explanations.

Where they may overlap

  • Paying attention to the person's story and symptoms.
  • Looking at patterns over time.
  • Considering context, routine and individual experience.

Where they differ

  • They use different concepts and explanatory models.
  • The strength and type of evidence behind claims can differ substantially.
  • A traditional explanation is not automatically evidence of clinical effectiveness.

Simple things you can consider.

These are general self-care ideas, not treatment instructions or cures.

Comfort & self-care

  • Keep hydration and meals reasonably regular.
  • Notice whether sleep or routine changes affect the symptom.
  • Observe possible triggers rather than assuming a cause.
  • Avoid things you already know reliably make you feel worse.

Safety first

  • Seek urgent medical help for sudden severe symptoms or rapidly worsening problems.
  • Examples include new one-sided weakness, trouble speaking, loss of consciousness or severe breathing difficulty.
  • When in doubt about a serious or worsening symptom, contact an appropriate healthcare professional.

What's missing?

Good health reasoning often depends on information that isn't in the first message.

  • When did it start?
  • How often does it happen, and how long does it last?
  • What else happens at the same time?
  • Any medicines, supplements, recent illness or major changes?
  • What makes it better or worse?
YOUR DECISION BRIEF

Make sense of it.

IHI's job is not to tell you what you have. Its job is to help you walk into a healthcare conversation with a clearer story.

  • Bring the symptom timeline.
  • Share what seems to trigger or relieve it.
  • Ask which explanations need to be considered.
  • Ask what information or tests would change the interpretation.
  • Ask which options are evidence-supported and what their risks are.

Over time.

A useful next version of IHI could let you keep a lightweight timeline of symptoms, observations and questions. A pattern can be worth noticing without proving that one thing caused another.
